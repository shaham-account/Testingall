## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, institutional, yet tangibly luxurious digital presence for a multi-division enterprise umbrella group. It bridges three distinct operational verticals:
1. **High-Velocity Creative & Media Production** (cinematic, dynamic, conversion-focused).
2. **Accredited Enterprise Compliance & Inspection SaaS** (rigorous, institutional, precision-engineered).
3. **Luxury Bespoke Corporate Gifting** (tactile, artisanal, high-touch executive tier).

The aesthetic marries **Corporate / Modern Authority** with **Refined Dark-Mode Glassmorphism** and surgical editorial contrast. Dark obsidian canvases evoke institutional gravitas, while deliberate shifts to crisp, stark-white container bands provide clinical legibility for compliance matrices, audit results, and enterprise dashboards. 

Every touchpoint balances technical exactitude with bespoke prestige. Users should feel an immediate sense of scale, financial stability, and operational perfection:
- **Audience:** Fortune 500 Procurement Directors, Chief Compliance Officers, VP-level Enterprise Brand Marketers, and Corporate Gifting Executives.
- **Atmosphere:** Controlled power, executive quiet luxury, rigorous security, and uncompromising craft.

## Colors

The color architecture is built upon high-contrast multi-division stratification. The palette anchors deep enterprise operations while providing division-specific cues without fragmenting the master corporate umbrella:

- **Neutral Base (Obsidian Slate):** `#0B0F19` serves as the foundational deep canvas, supported by `#111827` (Elevation Layer 1 / Cards) and `#1E293B` (Structural Outlines & Ghost Dividers). `#334155` provides secondary borders and passive icons.
- **Enterprise Executive Primary:** `#2563EB` (Interactive Indigo) and `#1D4ED8` (Pressed / Focus Indigo) anchor the Compliance and Inspection SaaS suites, system-wide interactive states, and core navigational actions.
- **Artisanal Luxury Accent (Warm Gold / Bronze):** `#D97706` and `#F59E0B` represent Bespoke Corporate Gifting and highlight high-tier cinematic case studies. Used selectively for high-touch CTA tiers, VIP ribbons, and luxury product callouts.
- **Accreditation & Trust (Emerald):** `#10B981` (with `#059669` hover states) is strictly reserved for verified institutional badges, compliance validations (e.g., IAR-112 USA Accredited), audit status indicators, and positive metric callouts.
- **High-Contrast Light Panels:** `#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC` create stark contrast bands for data-dense tables, audit readouts, sample design mockups, and corporate whitepapers. Text on light panels transitions to `#0F172A` with `#475569` secondary body copy.

## Typography

The typographical pairing unites **Plus Jakarta Sans** for prominent, high-authority architectural headings with **Inter** for dense, precision-driven user interfaces, micro-copy, and enterprise navigation:

- **Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Tightly tracked display titles (`-0.03em` to `-0.01em`) that deliver crisp geometric authority. The letterforms convey contemporary institutional strength without the sterile feel of legacy corporate grotesque fonts.
- **Body & Micro-Copy (Inter):** Highly legible across dark obsidian backgrounds. Inter's tall x-height and neutral geometry eliminate optical fatigue in dense data tables, compliance clause displays, and nested mega-menus.
- **Hierarchy Rules:** 
  - Division headers must use `headline-lg` or `headline-md` with `font-weight: 600` or `700`.
  - Mega-menu primary labels leverage `label-nav`, directly followed by `label-micro` running at 65% opacity for sub-nav descriptive context.
  - Verification tokens and performance tags strictly leverage `label-badge` with uppercase transformation and `0.06em` letter spacing.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an adaptive 12-column fixed-max grid (max-width: 1440px) pinned against an obsidian viewport. Margins and gutters expand gracefully to preserve structural cohesion across device tiers:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid, `margin: 3rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Section vertical padding follows an alternating cadence (`space-xl` × 2 to 3) to allow media showcases and tabular compliance tools room to breathe.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column grid, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`. Two-column mega-menus collapse into sequential stacked segment blocks.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column grid, `margin: 1.25rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Horizontal scrolling ribbons are deployed for division metrics and accreditation badges to conserve vertical viewport height.

Consistent spatial cadence applies across all inner component groups:
- Standard internal card padding: `space-lg` (1.5rem).
- Tight contextual chip and badge gaps: `space-xs` (0.25rem) to `space-sm` (0.5rem).
- Division switcher and segmented controls: `space-xs` internal gap between tab items.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages a hybrid model of **Translucent Glassmorphism**, **Crisp Structural Ghost Borders**, and **Deep Obsidian Surface Stacking**:

1. **Canvas Base (`#0B0F19`):** Root background, flat and non-reflective.
2. **Surface Tier 1 (Cards, Content Blocks):** `#111827` overlaid with a hairline 1px border of `#1E293B`. No blur. Low-diffused shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`.
3. **Glassmorphic Navigation & Floating Bars:** `rgba(11, 15, 25, 0.78)` background paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a bottom edge border of `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Ensures zero visual occlusion when traversing rich media.
4. **Mega-Menu Dropdown Architecture:** `rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.95)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`, framed by a crisp border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, casting an ambient shadow: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`.
5. **Interactive Glows & Hover Depth:** Primary interactions generate subtle radial ambient halos rather than heavy drop shadows:
   - SaaS/Inspection interactive targets: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.25)`.
   - Luxury Gifting interactive targets: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.22)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a **Soft (Level 1)** structural silhouette. Sharp corporate borders reflect institutional rigor, while subtle corner easing prevents visual fatigue:

- **Standard Buttons, Inputs, and Menu Links:** `0.25rem` (4px). Conveys technical precision and enterprise security.
- **Cards, Modals, and Mega-Menu Containers (`rounded-lg`):** `0.5rem` (8px). Keeps multi-column nested elements structured without unwanted bubble aesthetics.
- **Accreditation Badges, Division Tabs, & Metric Pills (`rounded-xl` / Full):** `9999px` (Pill format reserved strictly for metadata chips, division status indicators, and security credentials).

## Components

### 1. Navigation Header & Mega-Menu
- **Header:** Sticky top, 72px height, glassmorphic obsidian background (`rgba(11, 15, 25, 0.78)`), backdrop blur 16px. Contains group brand crest, multi-division root links, accreditation trust indicator, and global enterprise inquiry CTA.
- **Mega-Menu Dropdown (2-Column Architecture):**
  - **Left Column (Primary Navigation Links):** 60% width. Rows featuring `label-nav` titles alongside `label-micro` descriptions (e.g., *Inspect360: Automated real-time ISO/OSHA facility verification*). Hover states highlight rows with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)` and a 2px left border accent in `#2563EB`.
  - **Right Column (Featured Division Showcase):** 40% width. Enclosed card (`#0B0F19`) framed by `#1E293B`. Features high-contrast case study imagery or division highlight, accompanied by a dynamic metric callout chip and direct action link.

### 2. Badges & Accreditation Tags
- **Structure:** Inline-flex, padding `4px 10px`, border radius `9999px`, typography `label-badge`.
- **Security & Compliance:** Background `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`, border `1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)`, text `#10B981`. Icon prefix: 12px shield-check (e.g., `IAR-112 USA Accredited`).
- **Artisan / Bespoke Tier:** Background `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.12)`, border `1px solid rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.3)`, text `#F59E0B` (e.g., `Free 48-Hr Mockup`).
- **Performance & Media:** Background `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.12)`, border `1px solid rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.3)`, text `#60A5FA` (e.g., `3.4x ROAS Boost`).

### 3. Interactive Division Tabs
- Horizontal segmented pill bar resting on `#111827` with a 1px `#1E293B` contour.
- Active state transitions smoothly to solid `#1E293B` with high-contrast `#FFFFFF` text and division-coded micro-dot (Blue for Compliance, Gold for Bespoke Gifting, Silver-White for Media).

### 4. Buttons
- **Primary SaaS / Corporate CTA:** Background `#2563EB`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #3B82F6`, radius `0.25rem`. Hover: `#1D4ED8` with subtle ambient blue glow.
- **Luxury / Bespoke Action CTA:** Background `#D97706`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #F59E0B`, radius `0.25rem`. Hover: `#B45309`.
- **Secondary / Ghost Outlined:** Background `transparent`, text `#F8FAFC`, border `1px solid #334155`. Hover: Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, border `#64748B`.

### 5. Input Fields
- Background `#0B0F19`, border `1px solid #1E293B`, radius `0.25rem`, padding `0.75rem 1rem`. Typography `body-md` in `#FFFFFF`. Placeholder in `#475569`.
- Focused state: Border `#2563EB`, outer outline `2px solid rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.2)`.

### 6. Cards & Metric Callout Chips
- **Cards:** Surface `#111827`, border `1px solid #1E293B`, internal padding `space-lg`. Hover activates a subtle top-border accent matching the respective division palette.
- **Metric Callout Chips:** Embedded data displays with bold numbers in `headline-md` and supporting micro-descriptions. Outlined with low-contrast ghost borders (`#1E293B`) to maintain crisp data density.
